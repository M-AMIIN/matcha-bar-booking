Questions

Anything else, email .

How much space do you need?

About two by three metres, a table if the venue has one, and a power socket within ten metres. We bring the bar, cups, ice and water.

How far do you travel?

Anywhere in . Further away is possible with a travel fee in the quote.

How far ahead should we book?

Summer weekends go first, so two to three months ahead is safe for a wedding. For other dates a few weeks is often enough. Send the form and we'll tell you if your date is free.

Can we taste the drinks before we book?

Yes. Couples can book a tasting for two in Oslo. It costs and comes off the final price if you book.

Do you have options without dairy or caffeine?

Oat and coconut milk are always on the bar. Matcha has caffeine, so for kids and pregnant guests we make half-strength drinks and a strawberry milk without matcha.

How does the deposit work?

A deposit paid by card holds your date and is taken off the final price. You get it back if you cancel more than 30 days before the event.

Can we choose the cups, colours and sign?

Yes. Tell us your colours and names in the form, and we suggest bows, cups and a sign to match.
