What we collect

When you send the enquiry form we receive your name, email address, phone number if you give it, the occasion, date, number of guests, location, package and anything you write in the message. When you pay a deposit, Stripe processes your card details. We never see or store your card number.

Why we use it

Only to answer your enquiry, send you a quote, and plan and deliver the event if you book. The legal basis is taking steps you ask for before an agreement is made, and then carrying out that agreement (GDPR article 6(1)(b)).

Where it is stored

Enquiries arrive in our email inbox at [email provider, for example Google Workspace]. [If you use Formspree: enquiries also pass through Formspree, a form service based in the USA.] Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not share your details with anyone else and never use them for advertising.

How long we keep it

If you don't book, we delete your enquiry within 12 months. If you book, we keep what the Norwegian Bookkeeping Act requires, such as invoices and receipts, for five years, and delete the rest within 12 months after the event.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics. It remembers your language choice in your own browser. The fonts are stored on this site, so nothing is loaded from Google.
